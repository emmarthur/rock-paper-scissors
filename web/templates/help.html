How to play

Rules, scoring, and what happens behind each round

Rules

Each round you and the computer secretly pick one of three moves at the same time:

  • Rock crushes scissors.
  • Paper covers rock.
  • Scissors cut paper.

Same move on both sides is a tie. The computer picks uniformly at random — no memory of past rounds.

Scoring

Wins, losses, and ties accumulate for your session in the browser. Use Reset score to start fresh. Scores are not stored on the server after you close the tab.

The countdown

After you pick a move, the app counts “1… 2… 3…” before revealing the computer’s choice — the same little pause that makes the terminal version feel like a real throw-down.
